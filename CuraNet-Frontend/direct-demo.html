SEPARATE MODULE

First Aid Measures & Remedies List

Educational reference only. Serious or worsening symptoms require professional medical help.
🩹

Minor Cuts & Scrapes

Control bleeding, gently clean, cover and monitor.

🔥

Minor Burns

Cool with clean running water; avoid ice and household substances.

👃

Nosebleed

Sit forward and pinch the soft part of the nose continuously.

🦶

Minor Sprain

Protect, rest, use a wrapped cool pack and elevate when practical.

🌡️

Fever Support

Rest, fluids, comfortable clothing and symptom monitoring.

☀️

Heat Exhaustion

Move to a cool place, cool the person and offer fluids if safe.

💫

Fainting

Check responsiveness and breathing, keep safe and monitor.

🫁

Choking

Get emergency help immediately when breathing is not effective.

⚠️

Severe Allergic Reaction

Recognize severe symptoms and seek emergency help promptly.

🌬️

Asthma Attack

Help with the person's own prescribed plan and seek help if severe.

💧

Dehydration

Rest and offer fluids when safe; watch for severe symptoms.

🦴

Suspected Fracture

Keep the area still and get professional assessment.

☣️

Poison Exposure

Get expert help; do not guess at home treatment.

🧠

Seizure

Clear hazards, protect from injury, monitor and seek help when needed.

🧑‍⚕️

Possible Stroke

Sudden face, arm or speech changes need emergency help immediately.

❤️

Possible Heart Attack

Serious chest symptoms require urgent emergency medical help.

🩸

Heavy Bleeding

Call emergency services and apply firm direct pressure while waiting.
